Comparison guide · Nuclear costs

The comparison is only useful if the units match.

Generation versus storage. Forecast versus result. Wholesale cost versus your electric bill.

The fastest way to make an energy comparison look impressive is to compare different things. The fastest way to make it useful is to make the units, dates and services match.

Our pro-solar argument should survive that test. So should any nuclear proposal.

Six comparisons to stop making

Misleading shortcut Compare this instead
A battery's GWh against a reactor's GW Power and duration, plus the ability to recharge or keep generating
Utility-scale solar cost against a rooftop loan payment The same customer, service, cost boundary and financing basis
A mature future reactor model against a first installed home battery Comparable maturity and delivery assumptions
Canadian program dollars against U.S. equipment dollars Common currency, price year and included costs
An operating reactor's running cost against a new-build project's total cost Either the decision to retain existing assets or the decision to build new ones
Solar at noon against uninterrupted industrial power The complete system needed to meet the same hourly demand and reliability target

A cheap megawatt-hour is not a complete electric bill

IRENA's global weighted solar PV cost for new utility-scale projects in 2025 was US$44/MWh. It describes a generation-cost benchmark, not a U.S. household tariff and not a guarantee of power in every hour. IRENA report

The bill also reflects the applicable tariff, delivery, fixed charges, taxes and other local rules. A rooftop proposal must be evaluated against that household's avoided purchases and export compensation, with financing and maintenance included.

Nuclear targets need the same accounting discipline

For a new reactor, ask whether a cost includes financing during construction, escalation, contingency, shared infrastructure, fuel, operations, waste and decommissioning. Do not add categories twice if the original estimate already includes them.

For an SMR model, ask whether it describes the first unit or a mature repeated build. Factory learning may be a reasonable hypothesis. It is not an observed cost reduction until actual production and construction demonstrate it. IEA nuclear analysis

Storage is a service, not free energy

A battery can shift electricity, support selected loads and supply useful power during a limited dispatch window. It also loses energy and has output, cycling and lifetime limits. Its value depends on what it charges from and which service it is designed to provide. DOE storage basics

Do not multiply a battery's power rating by every hour of the year and call that annual generation. Do not assume a four-hour fleet solves a multi-day energy shortage. Equally, do not dismiss a two-hour peak service because it is not a year-round generator; the service may still be valuable.

Ask for two household cases

Request a “without the change” and a “with the change” projection using the same consumption, tariff and time horizon. The proposal should show:

  • Complete cash cost and any financed total of payments.
  • Production and usage assumptions, including shading and expected degradation.
  • Export credits, fixed charges and tariff-change sensitivity.
  • Replacement, maintenance and service responsibilities.
  • Verified eligibility for any assumed incentives.
  • Backup performance separately from bill savings.
  • Grid-services compensation separately from both, without double-counting energy.

Do not treat a tax credit as a cash discount available to everyone. Eligibility, timing and legal rules require current verification for the actual owner and transaction.

The practical conclusion

The best household option is not selected by the loudest national headline. It is selected by a working system, a credible proposal and the family's own priorities.

We favor local solar and storage because homeowners can evaluate real equipment and real services now. We do not need to pretend that every roof, loan or battery program is a good deal to make that case.
