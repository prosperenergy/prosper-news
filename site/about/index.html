What we are arguing

A future nuclear project is not a sound reason to ignore underperforming solar or postpone evaluating suitable storage. We challenge new-build costs, delivery risk, waste obligations and unclear household benefits. We acknowledge nuclear’s low-carbon output and real operating SMR examples.

How to read our evidence

  • Observed result: a completed milestone, operating outcome or measured event, within its stated source limits.
  • Target or budget: a forward-looking statement, not a delivered result.
  • Scenario: arithmetic or modeling conditional on inputs; not a forecast or offer.
  • Association: a statistical relationship that does not itself establish cause.
  • Opinion: our interpretation or recommendation, separated from the underlying record.

We link primary sources where possible, identify marketplace and industry data, include relevant contrary findings and show calculation assumptions. The library is curated, not an exhaustive systematic review. Research and project status can change after a review date; updates are editorial, not continuous monitoring.

What we will not claim

We do not claim that every nuclear plant causes nearby cancer, that no SMRs have operated, that batteries generate electricity, that solar and storage are risk-free, or that every household will save money. We do not claim that Prosper currently operates a VPP or offers a guaranteed grid-service payment.
