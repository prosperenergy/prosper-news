The Prosper next step

Make your solar work.
Then put storage to work.

A nuclear announcement does nothing for an underperforming system on your roof. Start with the power you already have, then build a storage plan around what your home actually needs.

01 / Check production

Is your solar doing its job?

Bring your monitoring history, recent alerts, and 12 months of electric bills. Compare production with the system design and seasonal conditions. A higher bill alone does not establish that the panels have failed.

Missing monitoring data can mean a communications problem; low output needs a qualified diagnosis. Keep roof access and electrical work with trained professionals.

02 / Size storage

Store power for a reason.

Decide whether your priority is evening energy use, managing time-of-use rates, or outage backup. Review surplus solar, usable battery capacity, power output, equipment compatibility, and the loads you want covered.

Prosper’s battery options include Tesla and Enphase. The final design, availability, installed cost, and backup duration depend on your home and proposal.

03 / Review the plan

Keep the whole system accountable.

Ask who will monitor the system, respond to alerts, support warranty claims, and explain battery settings. Make sure the proposal identifies equipment, installation scope, utility requirements, and costs.

Keep your monitoring access and system documents together. Confirm the planned backup configuration before relying on it during an outage.
