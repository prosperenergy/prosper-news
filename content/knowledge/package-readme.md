## The standpoint

Working solar and appropriate storage are practical household options now. A future reactor announcement is not a reason to leave existing solar underperforming or postpone a property-specific storage decision. Challenge new nuclear's costs, delivery and obligations with documented evidence.

The package does not claim that nuclear is imaginary, that every reactor causes cancer, or that batteries generate electricity. Health associations are distinguished from causation. Battery capacity is distinguished from continuous generation, and cost scopes and currencies are identified.

The national baseline is 2.91 million incremental batteries, 13.5 kWh each, totaling 39.285 GWh. At US$15,647 installed per home the illustration is US$45.533 billion before incentives. Existing storage is not subtracted from the residential-base proxy; original solar cost is excluded. Read the complete assumptions and limitations before quoting the result.
