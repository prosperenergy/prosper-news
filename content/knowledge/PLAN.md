# Prosper News knowledge base

Scope: expand the existing news.prospershield.io site; preserve its prior article and assets; no changes to mail DNS, CRM, credentials, customer forms or other sites. Craig explicitly requested the expansion and a research-package email to Zac.

Audience: homeowners with solar payments, homeowners considering storage, and people responding to claims that future SMRs make present solar decisions obsolete.

Five content pillars: nuclear cost/delivery; health/waste; solar performance; battery storage; distributed power control. Each connects documented evidence to a household decision. Searchable guides supply direct answers; the founder editorial provides a shareable point of view.

Priority hub and spokes:

- /: searchable article index and five-topic navigation.
- /guides/mini-nuclear-is-a-promise/: Craig's supplied brief, integrated with power-control and national battery scenarios.
- /guides/nuclear-project-scorecard/: project status, costs, schedules, and how to challenge forecasts.
- /guides/nuclear-health-evidence/: cancer studies, radiation exposure, accidents and research limitations.
- /guides/solar-performance/: homeowner production/service checklist.
- /guides/battery-backup/: load/energy distinctions, safety and a backup-duration calculator.
- /guides/half-of-solar-homes/: national storage scenario and editable cost/capacity calculator.
- /guides/who-controls-your-power/: business change and contract questions.
- /guides/fair-energy-comparisons/: equivalent-service, lifecycle and finance comparison guide.
- /guides/waste-and-water/: uranium, spent fuel and responsible solar/battery end-of-life.
- /guides/questions-and-answers/: common objections and plain-language glossary.
- /sources/: searchable source register with publication/check dates, evidence type and limitations.
- /about/: commercial-interest disclosure, method, correction contact, and editorial boundaries.
- /archive/solar-is-the-smarter-bet/: preserved prior published article, labelled as archive.

Not included: invented testimonials, unsupported all-reactors cancer claims, universal savings, guarantees of grid-service revenue, or a claim that Prosper already operates a VPP. Craig's autobiographical installation figures remain author-provided, not independently audited.

Design: reuse Prosper teal, existing Fander/Geist fonts, official logo, and supplied conceptual solar/storage image; an editorial reading layout with generous type, clear provenance, topic navigation and modest hover/focus effects. No generated media or paid services.

Verification: static build; all internal pages/anchors/assets; source URLs and external CTA checks; 320/390/768/1024/1440 layouts; keyboard, no-JS and reduced-motion paths; search/filter/no-results; calculator boundary cases; copy/share actions; OG/Twitter/canonical metadata; preview then production hash/readback. Release must restore existing deployment lock.
