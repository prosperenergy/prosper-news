UNDERSTAND YOUR BILL

What makes up your electric bill?

This clip breaks down an example bill: electricity supply, delivery and a customer charge. The example shows $69.70 for supply and $87.97 for delivery, including a $19.81 customer charge.

Some customer charges are fixed, while other delivery charges can vary with electricity use. Solar may reduce some usage-based costs; remaining charges depend on the utility’s tariff, your usage and the credit rules.
